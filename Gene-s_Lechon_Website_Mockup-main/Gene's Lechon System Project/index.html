About Gene's Lechon

For over 20 years, Gene's Lechon has been serving the best roasted pig in Northern Mindanao. Our secret family recipe and traditional cooking methods ensure every bite is perfectly crispy and flavorful.

We use only the freshest local ingredients and follow strict quality standards to deliver authentic lechon experience for all occasions - from family gatherings to grand celebrations.
